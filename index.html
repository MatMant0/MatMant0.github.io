Summer Blouse Performance and Pricing Diagnostics Dashboard

Group project (Group 12) · Tableau · ZARA sales data, 2018–2022 · Jan - April 2026

Summer blouses sold poorly compared with other categories. I analyzed ZARA sales data to find out why, and turned the findings into an interactive dashboard.

The question

Is the weak performance caused by price, stock, timing, or something else?

What I did

Built a four-view Tableau dashboard on 660 ZARA products: yearly blouse trend, seasonal sales breakdown, summer pricing diagnostics, and summer stock diagnostics.

What I found

Average blouse sales per item fell from 320 units in spring to 267 in summer, while jeans (313) and skirts (301) held up better. The highest-priced summer item was a blouse, at $149.88.

My role

Data cleaning, brainstormed regarding what parameters to use and how to use them, created all four views.

TikTok Claim vs Opinion: Engagement Analysis and Classifier

Course project · Python · pandas, scikit-learn · Jan - April 2026

I compared how claim-based and opinion-based videos perform on TikTok, then built a model that predicts which type a video is from its likes, comments, and shares.

The question

Does the algorithm reward claim videos differently, and is engagement enough to tell the two apart?

What I did

Removed 298 videos with missing data, trimmed the top 1% of outliers, tested the view difference with a Welch t-test, then trained logistic regression and random forest models on a 70/15/15 split.

What I found

Claim videos averaged about 484,000 views versus about 5,000 for opinion videos. The random forest reached 99.47% test accuracy; logistic regression reached 98.90%.

Dataset

Claim status and engagement data: 19,382 videos, 12 columns, 19,084 usable after cleaning.
